### Process Owner call prompts (select and adapt)

1. What is this process trying to achieve and what counts as completed?
2. Can we walk through one real code review, test, benchmark, documentation review or report task?
3. Which expert decisions, standards, control points or iterations matter?
4. Where is the material effort, rework, delay, inconsistency or risk, and what evidence supports it?
5. What tools and data already exist, and what restrictions apply?
6. What outcome is worth improving, how could we measure it and what must not worsen?
7. Who must accept the business result, and what should FDE investigate next?

**Remember:** One Canvas feeds both (a) per-opportunity Stage 4 handovers and (b) the parent-priority one-page SLT Discovery Update. It is a discovery aid, not a permission to upload restricted artefacts to an AI tool.
